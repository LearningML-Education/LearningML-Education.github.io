**The National Institute of Educational Technologies and Teacher Training (INTEF), through its [CodeINTEF](http://code.intef.es/) project, offers us [educational resources](http://code.intef.es/recursos/mas-recursos/) on Computational Thinking, programming and robotics. These are some of them devoted to AI and ML.**

[Artificial intelligence in the classroom with Scratch 3.0](http://code.intef.es/inteligencia-artificial-en-el-aula-con-scratch-3-0/). If you still don't know much about Machine Learning and want to get into its study, this resource will come in handy. It provides a complete and didactic introduction to Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, a virtual assistant is built with Scractch in several video tutorials and is completed with a great deal of information and resources on Artificial Intelligence. In the videotutorials we use [Machine Learning For Kids](https://machinelearningforkids.co.uk/), a fantastic tool that has inspired LearningML.

**[Can we trust the AI?](http://code.intef.es/podemos-fiarnos-de-la-inteligencia-artificial/)**

**Machine Learning is not 100% accurate.** Moreover, its results depend very much on how good the data set used to teach the machine is. This gives rise to a problem known as bias, explained in this resource, and which gives rise to major ethical dilemmas.

**[We learn to teach a computer with Teachable Machine V2](http://code.intef.es/teachablemachinev2/).**

This resource presents a tool, called Teachable Machine, with which you can easily get started in the world of Machine Learning.
