Conference on the teaching and learning of mathematics ["Creating teaching networks"](https://innovacionyformacion.educa.madrid.org/actividades/jornadas-ensenanza-aprendizaje-matematicas-creando-redes-docentes-i0069). Organised by the Directorate of Bilingualism and Teaching Quality in collaboration with the ‘Emma Castelnuovo’ Society of Mathematics Teachers of Madrid (SMPM). At the Instituto Superior Madrileño de Innovación Educativa (ISMIE).

It has been **a pleasure and a real privilege** to participate in this meeting representing **LearningML**. Sharing ideas on **how artificial intelligence can be meaningfully integrated into the teaching of mathematics and enhance computational thinking from the classroom.**

Thanks to the organisation for opening this **space for meeting, reflection and learning** among teachers, where knowledge becomes a network, and educational innovation becomes a reality.

We took away with us inspiration, challenges and a great desire to continue collaborating to **transform education** through technology, creativity and working together.

**Let's keep growing and connecting those of us who believe in open, critical and purposeful education!**
