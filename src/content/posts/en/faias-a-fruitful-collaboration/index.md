It is with some sadness that I write this blog post **to end the FAIaS Project (Fostering Artificial Intelligence at School), which has been a major breakthrough for LearningML.** FAIaS is an Erasmus+ project funded by the European Commission **whose purpose was to bring the use and understanding of artificial intelligence closer to schools.** It started in September 2020 and has recently ended in August 2023.

#### And what better tool to carry out this adventure than LearningML?

In recent years, the FAIaS project **has made many contributions to LearningML,** from developing **new features to creating materials and video tutorials** about the tool.

But perhaps the most relevant contribution has been the work to disseminate LearningML and involve educators. Thanks to the FAIaS project, we now have a large number of video tutorials on the tool, but **more importantly, we have many teaching activities created by and for teachers.** There are now more than a hundred educational activities on a wide range of subjects (from mathematics to Greek) for all ages. We have activities for primary, secondary, high school, special education, and even vocational training students.

I am confident that **the collaboration between LearningML and the FAIaS project has been a success.** The tool has matured significantly and is now a valuable resource for teachers and students. I am very grateful for the opportunity to have worked with such a talented and dedicated team. Surely, many of those who have collaborated under the umbrella of the FAIaS project will continue to be seen here.

**Thank you and we'll see you around!**
