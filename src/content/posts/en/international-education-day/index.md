The main objective of our association, as its name says, is the promotion and development of artificial intelligence and computational thinking in the educational world. That is why one of the activities proposed in the statutes is the ‘organisation of events and workshops for the promotion and dissemination of artificial intelligence and computational thinking’.

On this important day for society as a whole, International Education Day, established on 24 January by UNESCO and the General Assembly of the United Nations, we have held an online event in its tribute: ‘Educate to create. Let's reflect together’.

We count on the generosity of pioneers and experts who work for a free, free and quality education; encouraging the development of computational thinking to contribute to form critical and curious generations, who question and analyse; eager to learn and enjoy knowledge; and thus combat an extreme technological use that tells us what, how and when to consume.
