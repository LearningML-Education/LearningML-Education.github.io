The [**Raspberry Pi Foundation**](https://www.raspberrypi.org/) has organized a series of **[fortnightly seminars](http://raspberrypi.org/computing-education-research-online-seminars/)** with the intention of addressing current issues in **compunting education research** which. Due to the confined situation we are living in at the moment, are conducted online. These seminars are taking place from May 5th to July 28th 2020. In them several researchers tell about the projects they are currently working on.

The news is that I have been lucky enough to tell what, together with some members of the [KGBLIII](http://kgblll.github.io) research group, we are doing with **LearningML to teach Artificial Intelligence at school,** as I was invited as a speaker for the [session on May 19th.](http://raspberrypi.org/computing-education-research-online-seminars/previous-seminars/#learning-ai-at-school-with-scratch-and-learningml)

#### How did the session develop?

I exposed the reasons we have to teach Artificial Intelligence in school. The **basics of Machine Learning** and the relationship it has with Artificial Intelligence. I showed the main features of LearningML. I made a demo on image recognition. And I presented the first results we have obtained for the research described in this paper.
