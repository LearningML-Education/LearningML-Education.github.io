At LearningML, we are excited to announce **the launch of our new accounts on X** (formerly Twitter), **LinkedIn and Instagram.** Why have we decided to expand to these platforms? The answer is simple: **we want to be closer to you!**

Each of these networks offers a unique opportunity to connect with our community in different ways. On **X ([@\_learningml\_](https://x.com/_learningml_)),** we will share news, AI developments, and educational resources. On **LinkedIn** **([https://www.linkedin.com/company/learningml/](https://www.linkedin.com/company/learningml/)),** we will focus on building a professional network, collaborating with experts and educators interested in integrating artificial intelligence into the classroom. Finally, on **Instagram** **([https://www.instagram.com/learningml\_asoc/](https://www.instagram.com/learningml_asoc/))**, we will showcase in a visual and engaging way how we use technology to inspire young people.

We invite you to follow us on these platforms to stay up to date with the latest news and, above all, to help us build a future where artificial intelligence is accessible to everyone.

**Follow us and be part of this exciting adventure!**
