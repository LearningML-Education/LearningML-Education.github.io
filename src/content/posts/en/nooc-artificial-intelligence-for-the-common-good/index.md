[**Inteligencia Artificial Para el Bien Común**](http://enlinea.intef.es/courses/course-v1:INTEF+IABienComun+2021_ED1/about) **is a nanocourse developed and facilitated by Jorge Lobo Martínez (@lobo\_tic) that is part of the [INTEF](http://intef.es) training offer.** Despite its brevity, it manages to give a fairly complete overview of Artificial Intelligence, how it is being used to improve society, its risks and even how to introduce Artificial Intelligence activities in the classroom.

Participants were given **three different levels of activities** to complete the course. Levels 2 and 3 proposed to perform a **practical activity with LearningML,** and some of the **results** have been these:

- **[AI FOR DETECTING SCHOOL BULLYING](http://zonamagica.es/2021/05/14/inteligencia-artificial-para-el-bien-comun/) (by JoseMiguelV).**

- **[AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM APPLIED TO GEOPOLITICAL RISKS IN AN EDUCATIONAL ENVIRONMENT](http://arteehistoriaviajandoporelmundo.com/riesgos-geopoliticos/) (by Amhr).**

- **[HEALTHY FOOD WITH AI](http://vimeo.com/549194709) (by carlosrojas)**

**I am very grateful** to my colleagues at INTEF and to Jorge Lobo, creator of the nanocourse, **for trusting in LearningML and supporting this project.** Undoubtedly, these things are the ones that give strength to continue maintaining and developing it.

If you want to know more about this nanocourse, **I recommend you** to read this post: **[NOOC Artificial Intelligence for the Common Good at a bird's eye view.](http://code.intef.es/a_vista_pajaro/)**
