On July 3, 2021, together with Jorge Lobo (@lobo\_tic), we presented LearningML at the **[Hybrid Knowledge](http://estoyenetopia.es/conocimientos-hibridos/) forum**, **dedicated to “the impact of the omnipresence of artificial intelligences in our societies”.** It was organized by **[Etopia](http://estoyenetopia.es)**, an art and technology center located in Zaragoza.

In the presentation, I made an outline of the technology with which I developed LearningML. **Jorge Lobo gave a magnificent demo of the tool, in which he combined Artificial Intelligence with educational Robotics** (minute 24:32).

https://youtu.be/T1hMeyKcDMo?list=PLnwsvvvKVz-lE4OOhWdtUxgthpbtp2-aQ

**I thank the organizers of this event** for the opportunity they have given us to present LearningML.
