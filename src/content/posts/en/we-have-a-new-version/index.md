We are pleased to announce the arrival of **LearningML2**!

A new version of our **educational platform for teaching Artificial Intelligence** that comes **loaded with improvements and new features** that make learning even **easier and more fun**.

#### Improvements in the model editor

- **Graphical interface:** more intuitive and attractive for an optimised user experience.

- **Integrated text editor:** now you can easily copy and paste text examples or numerical data, without having to add them one by one!

- **Upload examples from file:** directly upload text files with examples, saving time.

- **Label editing:** modify label names to your liking.

- **Information pills:** learn about AI while the model is being generated.

- **Generation time visualisation:** see how long it takes to build your model.

- **Enhanced advanced mode:** each stage of training has a full screen, making it easier to focus on learning.

- **Save and load models:** you can save models to disk and load them from lml-scratch at any time.

#### And in the lml-scratch programming editor?

- **Single block for classification and confidence:** a single block to classify any type of model (text, image or numbers).

- **Model building in Scratch:** create the model directly in Scratch, without the need for the model editor.

- **Model information button:** consult the details of the loaded model.

- **Load models from files:** models generated with the editor can be loaded and shared.

- **Full integration with Scratch:** when you save your Scratch project (.sb3), the model is also saved for future sessions.

**We will be gradually offering tutorials with videos to teach you how to use this new version. Follow us on X, Instagram and Linkedin so you don't miss anything!**

If you are already a LearningML user or even if you are just starting out, we invite you to try **LearningML2** at https://v2.learningml.org. Do you encounter any problems or have any suggestions? Don't hesitate to report it through our contact form at https://web.learningml.org/contacto. **Your feedback is key to keep improving!**
